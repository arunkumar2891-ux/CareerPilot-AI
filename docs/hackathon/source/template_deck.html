Problem Statement: Job seekers repeat search, resume tailoring, and tracking for every opening, and generic AI shortcuts fabricate experience they cannot defend.

Problem Statement
01  Search is repetitive

Listings live on job boards. A candidate re-runs the same searches every day, skims duplicates, and loses track of what they have already seen.

02  Tailoring is slow

Each role needs a resume rewritten against its description and shaped to pass an applicant tracking system (ATS), usually by hand.

03  Tools fabricate

Generic AI resume tools raise keyword scores by inventing employers, metrics, and skills, leaving a document the candidate cannot defend in an interview.

Who feels it: mid-career technology professionals in India, and those targeting US roles, who apply every week and keep a master resume in a Google Doc.
The cost is not only time. It is applying to the wrong roles, or sending a resume that does not hold up.

Google AI Models
In production

Gemini API

gemini-3.6-flash scores each job against the master resume, rewrites the resume into the ATS contract, drafts cover letters, and writes interview briefs. A second Gemini key is the automatic fallback.

Planned

Gemini Omni

Read an uploaded PDF resume or a screenshot of a job posting into the same master-resume corpus, so setup needs no retyping.

Planned

Gemini Live

Spoken interview rehearsal built on the brief the API already writes, with follow-up questions drawn from the candidate's real resume.

Why a Flash-class model: the match gate runs on every discovered job, and the rewrite runs only on jobs that pass it, so per-job cost stays low enough for a daily scheduled run.
Business Model, Competition and Markets
Business model
  • Free: manual search, small monthly quota of tailored resumes
  • Pro: scheduled daily runs, more roles, ATS review, cover letters, interview prep, apply by email
  • Credits: top-ups for heavy months
  • Teams: per active candidate, for bootcamps and placement cells
Competition
  • Teal, Simplify: tracking and tailoring, triggered by hand
  • LazyApply, Sonara: volume auto-apply, not defensibility
  • Jobscan, Rezi: keyword scores, no discovery or tracking
  • Naukri, Instahyre: discovery only
  • Us: the whole loop, gated by match score, validated against the source
Target markets
  • Primary: India. India-based and India-remote roles
  • Secondary: United States, already the default search location
  • Next: United Kingdom, Canada, Singapore on the same English-language LinkedIn pipeline
