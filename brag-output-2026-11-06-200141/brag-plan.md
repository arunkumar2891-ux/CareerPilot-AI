# Brag Plan: CareerPilot AI

## What is this app?
CareerPilot AI is an autonomous job-search workspace. It discovers roles, scores the match against a master resume, tailors the resume, and tracks every application on a board.

## The angle
The same quiet mission-control film, rebuilt for a phone. The aircraft still climbs. Under it, the board is a vertical stack: Discovered, then Resume Ready, then Applied. The card drops one row, the way a thumb would scroll the board.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The lockup's aircraft, centered in the tall frame. Then the line, wrapped: "The search shouldn't be a second job."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The dashboard sentence, verbatim, stacked under a DASHBOARD label. Three words arrive down the column: Discovered, Scored, Tailored.
- A sample card, Northwind Labs / Staff Engineer, drops from the Discovered row into Resume Ready. Those labels are the real board. The company, title, and match number 94 are fictional stand-ins.
- The match stamp lands, then the resume contract's SUMMARY header. Resume Ready means a tailored resume is ready to send.

## Outro / punchline
The lockup, still, in the center of the frame. "CareerPilot AI." Then the site's claim: "Scores your match. Tailors the resume."

## User flow worth showing
Entry: the dashboard command center. Key action: a discovered job is scored. Result: the card drops into Resume Ready with a tailored resume.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet mission-control product film, framed for a phone
- Interpretation: four scenes, long holds, mixed case, type that fits a 1080-wide column. No side-by-side kanban. The move is vertical.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#0B120E`
- Accent: `#3DA988` (`--brand-jade`). Forest `#14603A` is the light primary and is not the mark.
- Text: `#F7F5F3`. Muted `#96A69D`. Card `#131B17`. Hairline `#26312C`.
- Display font: Inter 700, standing in for Inter Tight, which Hyperframes does not bundle.
- Body font: Inter. Status labels: JetBrains Mono.
- Strongest visual element: the aircraft lockup, then the board restacked as three full-width rows.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (109.96 BPM). Cue preset: `.agents/skills/brag/assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`
- Music treatment: bed from 0, volume about 0.34, short fade-in, fade under the lockup.
- Music cue guidance: strong cues at 8.74s (third status word), 13.11s (match stamp), 17.47s (wordmark). Status words on every other beat: 6.56, 7.64, 8.74. The card starts its drop on 11.46.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. The jade glow steps with extracted bass at those three cues (about 0.54, 0.55, 0.57). No waveform, no idle pulse.
- SFX posture: three soft sounds. Accent on the hook, a drop as the card leaves, a warm impact as the match lands.
- Audio-coupled moments: the three words, the vertical card move, the match stamp, the wordmark.
- Restraint rule: no stacked hits, no voice ducking.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Climb — 4.2s
Centered aircraft and baseline. The hook wraps to two lines and holds.
Sequential/interaction: the line arrives after the mark, which is already on frame 0.
Audio intent: quiet bed, one soft accent as the line lands.
Audio-coupled idea: none beyond that accent. Opening bass is nearly silent, so the glow stays dim.
Music: warm bed, low.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Command — 6.2s
DASHBOARD, then "Your autonomous job search command center." Then three full-width rows: Discovered, Scored, Tailored.
Sequential/interaction: yes. The rows arrive at 6.56, 7.64, and 8.74 and stay.
Audio intent: the third row meets the first loud beat. The glow opens.
Audio-coupled idea: three short labels, every other beat, then a hold of the stack.
Music: bed lifts at 8.74s.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Board — 5.6s
JOB DISCOVERY. Three rows stacked: Discovered, Resume Ready, Applied, with the real column hints. The sample card sits in Discovered, drops into Resume Ready on the 11.46 beat, then the match stamp lands on 13.11. SUMMARY follows.
Sequential/interaction: yes. A downward move, then the score, then the section header.
Audio intent: a soft drop as the card leaves, a warm impact as the score lands.
Audio-coupled idea: the drop on the beat grid, the score on the strong cue.
Music: bed stays under the move.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Lockup — 4.0s
The aircraft, then CareerPilot AI, then "Scores your match. Tailors the resume." Held to the end.
Sequential/interaction: wordmark, then the claim.
Audio intent: the bed fades. The wordmark meets 17.47. No extra hit.
Audio-coupled idea: wordmark on the strong cue. The glow takes its last step.
Music: fade through the hold.
Transition mood: none.
